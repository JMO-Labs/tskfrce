Goal complete

Not on screen, still included

  • Built-in goals: extra screen time, and a "Get out of jail free" card that cancels a penalty. Each is off until a parent allows it.
  • Goal settings: how many goals per kid (default by age mode), whether points go in by hand or automatically, withdrawals on or off, kid suggestions on or off, and whether kids see sibling contributions.
  • Withdrawals are blocked once a goal is full and waiting on a parent. Family goal points never come back out.
  • Penalties can take from a family goal or raise its target.
