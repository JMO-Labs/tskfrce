# Time banking (saved screen time)

Time banking lets a kid keep the screen time they didn't buy today. The saved minutes add to tomorrow's room, so they can save up for a bigger day.

## Who it's for

Families that use [screen time](../04-everyday-use.md#rewards-and-screen-time) (kids spend points on extra internet minutes) and want kids to plan ahead instead of buying every day.

## Turn it on or off

1. Open **Kids & chores → Rewards → Screen time**. Make sure the switch at the top is **On**.
2. Open the **Save unused time** row.
3. Flip **Carry unused minutes over**.
4. Optional: set **Save up to (min)**. It goes in steps of 15, from 15 to 480. Leave it empty to use each kid's daily max.

The row's summary shows **Off**, **Needs a max**, **On**, or **On · up to N min**.

## Step by step

1. In the **Max per day** row, pick a limit (for example 60 min). Banking needs a max, from the family or from a single kid's own limit. Without one, the row says "Pick a max per day first, so there is something to save up to."
2. Turn on **Carry unused minutes over**.
3. Each day at the daily reset, the minutes a kid could have bought but didn't are added to their saved time.
4. Look at **Kids** on the same page: each kid shows "saved N min" under their points.

Example: max 60 min and a kid buys 15. The 45 left over are saved. Tomorrow the kid can buy up to 105 min.

## What your kid sees

In **Rewards → Screen time** the kid sees "You saved N min — today you can buy up to N min." They still pay points, and the button still says **Request screen time** (or **Get screen time** if you turned on **Approve automatically**).

## Limits and gotchas

- Saved time is room to buy, not free time. Points and your approval work as usual.
- The cap is **Save up to (min)**, or one day's max if empty. If you type less than the largest daily max, the page warns you to use at least that.
- A kid with no daily max saves nothing.
- Turning it off clears all saved time. Turning it on again starts from zero.
- If you turn **Screen time** itself off, the saved settings are hidden but kept.
- Bought minutes still never work during off hours like bedtime.

## FAQ

**Does saved time run out?** It stays until the kid buys with it or you turn banking off, but never goes above the cap.

**Why does my kid's saved time look smaller than before?** You may have lowered **Save up to**. A kid can only use the saved time up to the cap.

**Can a kid buy it all at once?** A request is 15 to 120 minutes, so a big day may take two requests.
