# Less parent work

Three optional helpers so you approve fewer chores by hand: **trust mode**, **bedtime auto-approve** and a **weekly recap**. Trust mode and bedtime auto-approve are off until you switch them on. The weekly recap is on by default.

## Who it's for

Families where most chores get approved anyway and the tapping has become a chore of its own. You stay in charge: you can spot-check, take points back and turn any of it off.

## Turn it on or off

| Helper | Where | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Trust mode | **Kids & chores → Kids → the kid** | **Trust** row, switch **Trust mode** |
| Always check | **Kids & chores → Chores → the chore → Options** | **Always check** |
| Bedtime auto-approve | **Settings → Family → Bedtime & recap** | **Bedtime auto-approve**, then **Approve at** |
| Weekly recap | **Settings → Family → Bedtime & recap** | **Weekly recap** |

You can also switch on trust mode and bedtime auto-approve in setup, under **Tweak** in the optional extras. A card on **Today** suggests each one when it fits.

## Parent walkthrough

### Trust mode

1. Open **Kids & chores**, pick a kid and open the **Trust** row.
2. Switch on **Trust mode**. The kid starts at **New**: you approve every chore.
3. After 10 approved chores the kid becomes **Trusted**. Chores now approve themselves, but about 1 in 5 waits for you as a **Spot check**.
4. After 30 more, the kid becomes **Proven**. About 1 in 20 is a spot check.
5. To set a level yourself, tap **Change**, pick **New**, **Trusted** or **Proven** and tap **Save**.

Chores that approved themselves show **Approved on its own** on **Today**. The last 7 days are in **Approved on their own** on the kid's page. They send you no alert.

### Wasn't done

If a chore approved itself but wasn't really done, tap **Wasn't done** on it. The points come back. If your kid already spent them, the balance stops at zero and nothing is owed. By default the kid also drops one level; untick **Also lower trust level** to keep it.

### Bedtime auto-approve

1. Open **Settings → Family → Bedtime & recap**.
2. Switch on **Bedtime auto-approve**.
3. Set **Approve at**, from 6:00 PM up to 15 minutes before the day resets.

If the switch is greyed out, move the day reset later than 6:15 PM first. The next morning you get one line in your inbox, like "Bedtime approved 3 chores."

### Weekly recap

Every Sunday at 7:30 PM you get "Your week at (your family)" in your inbox. Open it from the message or from **More → Weekly recap**. **Worth a look** shows up to 3 things, most serious first, and **See all** shows the rest. Under **This week** tap a kid for their numbers. If all is fine it says "All good this week."

## What the kid sees

- A small level chip on their **Me** page (New, Trusted or Proven).
- Chores that approve themselves pay points as usual.
- A spot check looks like any chore waiting for a parent.
- If you tap **Wasn't done**, a gentle message.

## Limits and gotchas

- Spot checks and **Always check** chores are never auto-approved, not even at bedtime.
- A team chore approves itself only if every kid on it is in trust mode, and it follows the lowest level.
- Bedtime auto-approve needs the day reset later than 6:15 PM.
- The recap goes to push and other notification places only if they already get the daily summary. Quiet hours hold it.
- Badges a kid already earned stay after **Wasn't done**.

## FAQ

**Does a kid know the spot check is random?** They see it as a chore waiting for you.

**Can I start a kid at Proven?** Yes. Tap **Change** and pick **Proven**.

**What happens to chores left waiting at the daily reset?** They stay in **Needs you**, labelled with their day, until you answer. Bedtime auto-approve also clears them if it is on.

**How do I stop all of this?** Switch off **Trust mode** on the kid, **Bedtime auto-approve** and **Weekly recap** in the places above.
