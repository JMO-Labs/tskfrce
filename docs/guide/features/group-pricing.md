# Prices per device group

You can charge more screen time points for some devices, like a game console, and less for others. This is set per **device group**.

> There is no setting to price single apps. Prices are per device group.

## Who it's for

Families who want a tablet or console to cost more than the rest.

## Turn it on or off

1. Open **Kids & chores → Rewards → Screen time** and make sure the switch is **On**.
2. Open the **Prices per device group** row.
3. Flip **Different prices for device groups**.

Turn it off and everything goes back to the one family price. Group prices are kept for later.

## Step by step

1. First set the family price in the **Price** row (**Points per 15 min**). It starts at 20.
2. Turn on **Different prices for device groups**, then tap **Set group prices ›**. This opens the groups on the **Devices** page.
3. Open a group (or make a new one) and open **Settings**.
4. Check that **Lock with the kids' chores** is on. Only chore-locked groups can have a price.
5. Type a number in **Screen time price** (1 to 1000). Leave it empty to use the family price, shown as "Family price (N)".

The group's summary then reads **Chores lock · N pts**.

## What your kid sees

When prices differ, **Rewards → Screen time** shows **What for?** with choices:

- **All my devices**: costs the highest price of the devices it covers.
- **Other devices**: devices in no priced group, at the family price.
- One choice per group, each showing "N pts / 15 min".

If every choice costs the same, the kid only sees the single normal choice.

## Limits and gotchas

- Groups with **Lock with the kids' chores** off are free of the chore check and are never sold.
- A device in two groups costs the higher price.
- A group choice opens only the devices in it that the paid price covers. A dearer device shared with another group stays locked.
- If you raise a group's price after a kid asked, the request still works for devices the price they paid covers.
- Bought minutes never work during off hours like bedtime.

## FAQ

**Can I price one app?** No. Price a device group instead.

**I don't see the Screen time price box.** Check that screen time is **On**, **Different prices for device groups** is on, and the group's chore lock is on.

**What if I leave the price empty?** The group uses the family price.
