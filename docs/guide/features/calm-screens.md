# Calm screens

The parent screens show a little at first. You open a row to see more. This came with version 2.8.0 and applies to every family. There is nothing to turn on or off.

## Who it's for

Every parent. It matters most if the old screens felt like too much at once, or you are looking for a setting that seems to have moved.

## Turn it on or off

You can't. Calm screens are how the parent app works now. Some pieces have their own switches, such as **Weekly recap** in **Settings → Family → Bedtime & recap**.

## Parent walkthrough

- **Today**: a **Needs you** bar when something waits, one card for each kid and a **Devices** line. The **…** at the top right has **Start Happy Hour**.
- **A kid's page**: points, level and a few rows. Open a row to see more and change it right there. Tap the points chip for points history. **Edit** and **More** are at the top; **More** has **Bonus**, **Deduct** and **View as kid**.
- **A chore**: rows for **Basics**, **Steps**, **Who & when** and **Options**. The chore's **…** menu has **Duplicate** and **Delete**. The type tile opens the notes kids can send.
- **A device**: rows for **Right now**, **Details**, **Network** and **Activity**.
- **Devices list**: **Home network** is the row at the top. It holds blocking new devices and the Home box status.
- **Settings → Family**: rows for **Style**, **Household**, **Bedtime & recap**, **Points rules**, **Minigames** and **Penalties**. Changes save as you go.
- **Notifications**: each row has one main button. **Send back**, **Decline** and **Dismiss** are in the row's **…** menu. **Settings → Notifications → When and how** holds quiet hours.
- **Setup step 3 (kids)**: each added kid is one line. The avatar grid opens from the avatar button.

## What the kid sees

Nothing changes on kid screens. Kids still pick their own celebrations on **Me**.

## Limits and gotchas

- If a form has several fields, an **Unsaved changes** bar shows **Cancel** and a save button. Leaving with unsaved edits asks **Discard changes?**; **Keep** puts you back in the field you were typing in.
- Family rows save as you go, with no Save button. A pair like bedtime auto-approve and its time saves once both are valid.
- Screen readers and keyboards: rows are labelled buttons, and phone controls are at least 44px tall.

## FAQ

**Where did X go?**
- Points history: the points chip on the kid's page.
- Block new devices and Home box status: **Devices → Home network**.
- Bedtime auto-approve and Weekly recap: **Settings → Family → Bedtime & recap**.
- Start Happy Hour: the **…** on **Today**.
- Send back, Decline, Dismiss: the **…** on the row.
- Delete a chore: the chore's **…** menu.

**Is anything missing?** No. Everything is still there, one tap further in.
