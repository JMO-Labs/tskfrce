# Homework mode

Homework mode opens only the school sites on a device that is paused, for a set time. Everything else stays blocked.

## Start it yourself

1. Open a paused device or a group.
2. Tap **…**.
3. Pick **Homework 30 min**, **1 hour**, **90 min** or **2 hours**.

The device's status chip now says **Homework**. Tap it and pick **Back to schedule** to end homework early.

## When your kid asks

A kid whose device is paused sees **Ask for homework time**. Their request shows up in **Needs you**.

- **Approve** gives 1 hour. The row's **…** has the other lengths.
- **Decline** says no. Your kid can ask again after 5 minutes.

## Choose the sites

- **Settings → Homework**: "Allowed for everyone" adds to the built-in list of school sites. "Never during homework" always wins.
- **Kids & chores**: pick a kid and open the **Homework sites** row to add sites for that kid only.

## See what was used

The **Homework activity** row shows which sites were used. From there you can block a site or add one.

## Limits

Big school sites share their servers with other sites (Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare). A determined kid can sometimes reach another site that sits on the same servers. Look in **Homework activity** for names you don't recognise, then block them or end homework early.
