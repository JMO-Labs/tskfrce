# Minigames

Kids earn quick games by doing chores and play them in the **Games** tab. Each game pays a few points.

**Who it's for:** kids who like a small reward right after a chore. You decide how games are earned and how many points they can add.

## Turn it on or off

Open **Settings → Family → Minigames**.

- **Minigames** turns games on or off for the whole family. It is on by default.
- Under **Per kid**, each kid has a **plays games** switch. Turn it off to hide the **Games** tab for that kid.

## Set the rules

With **Minigames** on you will see:

- **Earn a game**: **Every chore** (default), **Once a day**, **Random** (about half of approved chores) or **Custom**.
  - With **Custom**, only chores where you turn on **Earns a game** in the chore editor give a game.
- **Game points cap**: the most points games can add each day, as a share of the chore points that kid earned that day. Pick 0% to 50% in steps of 5. The default is 10%. At 0%, games add no points.
- **Family leaderboard**: shows or hides the weekly board.

## What your kid sees

1. After an approved chore, a card says **You earned a game!** with **Play now** or **Save it**.
2. The **Games** tab shows how many games are saved and a **Play** button. With none saved it says "Finish a chore to earn a game."
3. A wheel picks the game. Games include Quick-fire, Hungry Cat, Math Dash, Pack the Box and Shelf Memory.
4. At the end they get up to 3 stars and any points earned.
5. The shelf shows stars for each game, and, if the board is on, "This week" with the family's stars.

The first time a kid opens **Games**, a short welcome says "Finish chores, earn games! Each game pays a few points."

## Limits and gotchas

- A kid can save up to **5** games. No new games are earned until they play some.
- Make-up chores for a penalty never earn a game.
- **Games sleep** at each kid's bedtime (the end of their last allowed window in **Hours**, until the day resets) and during your family **Quiet hours** (**Settings → Notifications → When and how**). Saved games wait. The kid sees "Games are asleep" and when they wake.
- The app decides the score, stars and points. A kid can't change them.
- If a kid hits the daily cap, they can still play. Games just add no more points that day.
- While you are viewing as a kid, the **Play** button is off. Only your kid can play.

## FAQ

**Why can't my kid play?** Check the family **Minigames** switch and the kid's own switch under **Per kid**. Then check that games aren't asleep.

**Can I stop games from giving points?** Set **Game points cap** to 0%.

**Which chores give a game?** Depends on **Earn a game**. For **Custom**, the chores with **Earns a game** switched on.
