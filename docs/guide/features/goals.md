# Goals

A goal lets a kid save points toward something bigger, like a bike, a game or a family trip. You set the price. Their meter fills as they save.

**Who it's for:** kids old enough to save up. You set how many goals each age group can have.

## Turn it on or off

Open **Kids & chores**, then **Rewards**, then **Goals**, then **Goal settings** at the bottom. Switch **Goals on** off and kids no longer see goals. (If it's off, the Goals page has a **Turn on** link.)

## Make a goal

1. In **Goals**, tap **+ New goal**.
2. Choose the type: **Custom**, plus **Extra screen time** and **Get out of jail free** when those are turned on.
3. For **Custom**, add an emoji, a **Name** and **Price, points**.
4. Under **Who**, tap one kid, several kids (each gets their own meter), or **Family** (one shared meter).
5. Tap **Create goal**.

## When your kid suggests one

If **Kids can suggest goals** is on, your kid can type an idea. It shows under **Waiting on you** (and on **Today**).

- Tap **Set price**, type a price and tap **Save price** to approve it.
- Or tap **…**, then **Decline**.

## When the meter fills

You get a notice: "… is full". Tap **Fulfilled** once you've given them the thing. The kid gets a mascot party the next time they open the app. A family goal parties for every kid.

An **Extra screen time** goal gives free minutes as soon as you tap **Fulfilled**, and the kid's devices unlock for that long. It needs screen time to be turned on. If the kid has no devices yet, you'll see a warning.

A **Get out of jail free** goal is a card the kid can use to cancel a penalty.

## Manage goals

- **Change price** and **Remove** are in the **…** on each goal. Removing gives every kid back what they saved.
- **+ Chip in** adds your own points to a family goal. They don't come from any kid.
- Finished goals sit under **Done**.

## Goal settings

Each change saves on its own.

- **Goals on**, **Kids can suggest goals**.
- **Kids can take points back** and **Kids see what siblings gave**.
- **Share chore points automatically** with **Share of each chore, %**. When on, that share of each approved chore goes to the goal the kid picked. Off means kids add points by hand.
- **Limits by age**: **Little kids**, **Kids**, **Teens**: how many goals each can have.
- **Built-ins**: **Extra screen time** (price and minutes) and **Get out of jail free** (price).

Defaults: suggestions on, taking points back on, siblings hidden, automatic saving off (25% when on), limits 1 / 2 / 3, and both built-ins off (extra screen time 200 points for 60 minutes, Get out of jail free 500 points).

## What the kid sees

On the **Rewards** tab, **My goals** shows each goal with a progress bar and "saved / price".

- **+ Add** opens a stepper in steps of 10, with **Add** and, if allowed, **Take back**. **Remove goal** is there too.
- **Auto-save here** picks the goal that gets the automatic share.
- **+ Suggest a goal** (or **+ Pick a goal** when suggestions are off) lets them type an idea or start a built-in.
- A full goal says "Goal reached!" and who it's waiting on.

## Limits and gotchas

- Saved points leave the kid's balance until the goal is fulfilled, removed or taken back.
- Family goals can't be taken back by kids.
- Only goals that are waiting, active or full count toward the age limit.
- **Extra screen time** is greyed out in settings until screen time is on.

## FAQ

**My kid can't add a goal.** They may be at the limit for their age, or **Kids can suggest goals** is off and no built-ins are turned on.

**What happens to the points if I remove a goal?** They go back to the kids who saved them.

**Does a family goal use my kids' points?** Yes, whatever each kid gives. **+ Chip in** adds yours.
