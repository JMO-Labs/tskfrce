# 2.8.0: Calm parent screens

## Changed for every family

- **Today opens calm.** The **Needs you** tab became a strip.
- **The kid page** has summary rows, and you edit in place.
- **Chore, device, group, notification, screen time, setup step 6 and custom badges screens** open calm, with less on screen at once.
- **Setup step 3 (kids) is calm.** Added kids show one line each, and the avatar grid opens from the avatar button.
- **The notifications inbox has one main button per row.** Send back, Decline and Dismiss are in the row's **…** menu. The filter counts are true counts.
- **Settings → Family is three rows** (Household, Bedtime & recap, Points rules) and saves as you go.
- **Rewards history** has a points-spent chip, a kid filter and an All / Rewards / Screen time switch.
- **Links and the Back button follow focus.**
- **Screen reader and keyboard:** summary rows are labelled buttons, a focus ring is visible in light and dark, and phone controls are at least 44px tall.

## Moved

- Points history is now the points chip.
- Start Happy Hour is in the Today **…** menu.
- The bell and your initial are at the top right on a computer too. The bell left the side bar.
- Chore notes are behind the type chip.
- Advanced is under **When and how**.
- Delete is in the **…** menu.
- Network (block new devices, Home box status) is now **Home network**, at the top of **Devices**.
- Extras is now **Settings → Family → Bedtime & recap**.
- Decline, Send back and Dismiss on notifications are in the row's **…**.

## Fixed

- Screen time names no longer wrap badly.
- The setup step bar fits on a phone.
- **Keep** in the unsaved-changes prompt puts you back in the field you were typing in.
- The Back button no longer skips the unsaved-changes prompt.
- The emoji picker closes after you pick.
- In the kid editor, screen readers now read out a bad PIN, and the PIN field is marked invalid until it has 4 digits.
- Guide pictures match the calm screens (Happy Hour, Wasn't done, group price, Bedtime & recap, setup owners).
