# 2.7.0: Less parent work

## Changed for every family

- **Chores waiting at the daily reset now stay waiting.** Before, a chore a kid had finished but you hadn't approved was dropped at the reset and the kid got nothing. Now it stays in **Needs you**, labelled with its day, until you answer. Approving it later pays as normal. Unfinished chores are still cleared at the reset, and, since 2.6.0, a day with a chore never done counts as missed for the streak.
- **Weekly recap is on by default**: Sundays at 7:30 PM, in your inbox. It goes to push and other notification places only if they already get the daily summary. Turn it off in **Settings → Family → Less tapping**.
- **Setup has an optional step 8**, "Optional extras".
- **The kid page header** has 3 buttons and a **More** menu (Bonus, Deduct).

## New and optional (off until you switch them on)

- **Trust mode**, per kid: levels New, Trusted and Proven, random spot checks (1 in 5 at Trusted, 1 in 20 at Proven), an **Always check** switch on each chore, and **Wasn't done** to take points back (never below zero) and lower the level.
- **Bedtime auto-approve**: chores still waiting approve themselves at a time you pick. Spot checks and **Always check** chores stay.
- Two new **Try this** cards, a **Less tapping** card in Settings, and a trust level chip on a kid's **Me** page.
