# 2.10.0: Goals

## New for every family

- **Kids save points toward something bigger,** like a bike, a game or a family trip. Goals sit at the top of **Rewards**. Kids tap **+ Add** to move points in.
- **Kids can suggest a goal.** A parent sets the price. A suggestion shows under **Waiting on you** and on **Today**.
- **Own goals or family goals.** A goal can be for one kid, for several kids (each gets their own meter) or for the whole family (one shared meter).
- **Chip in.** Parents can add their own points to a family goal. They don't come from any kid.
- **A mascot party.** When a meter fills, you get a notice. Tap **Fulfilled** and the kid gets a party the next time they open the app. A family goal parties for every active kid.
- **Fair by default.** Saved points leave the kid's balance. Removing a goal gives every kid back what they saved.
- **Two built-in goals:** extra screen time (needs screen time banking) and **Get out of jail free**.

## Settings

- **Rewards → Goals → Goal settings:** Goals on or off (on by default), whether kids can suggest goals or take points back, whether kids see what siblings gave, automatic saving (a share of each approved chore goes to the kid's chosen goal), how many goals each age can have, and the built-in goals.
