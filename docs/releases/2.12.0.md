# 2.12.0: Minigames

## New for every family

- **Kids earn a quick game for each approved chore.** A mascot pops in with "You earned a game!". Kids play now or save it (up to 5, they never expire).
- **A mascot wheel picks the game:** a 60-second quick-fire run of 20 tiny house games, Hungry Cat, Math Dash (adding, times tables or fractions by age), Pack the Box, or Shelf Memory.
- **Upgrades and power-ups.** Stars unlock three permanent upgrades per game (like a bigger bowl or extra time) that make the start of a round a little easier. The wheel sometimes gives a power-up for next time (like Magnet or 50/50).
- **Rounds pay tickets that turn into points.** Game points are capped at 10% of the day's chore points, so games never replace chores.
- **Stars, personal bests, five new badges** and a weekly family board, compared only within each age group.
- **Games sleep at bedtime and in quiet hours.** Saved games wait until morning.
- **Make-up chores earn no games.**

## Settings

- **Settings → Family → Minigames:** on or off, how games are earned (every chore, once a day, random, or chosen chores), the points cap (0 to 50%), the family board, and on or off per kid.
- **Custom:** the chore editor has an **Earns a game** switch.
